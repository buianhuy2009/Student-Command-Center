2. Google User Data & Requested Scopes (Least Privilege)

When you connect your Google Workspace or personal Google Account, StudentOS requests only the minimum scopes required for core academic organization (reduced from 13 down to 6 scopes). We never request broad Drive or full Gmail write access.

OAuth 2.0 Scope Type Purpose Sent to AI?
classroom.courses.readonly Non-sensitive Read enrolled course names and subjects to organize your dashboard. Does not read student rosters or grades. No
classroom.coursework.me.readonly Non-sensitive Read coursework titles, descriptions, and teacher-set due dates for your enrolled classes. No (structured dates are processed locally)
classroom.announcements.readonly Non-sensitive Read teacher announcements to find assignments and deadlines posted in announcement feeds. Yes (announcement text sent to Gemini/Groq to parse dates)
calendar.events Non-sensitive Read timetable events to prevent study clashes, and add study blocks when you tap "Add to Calendar". Never deletes existing personal events. No
drive.file Non-sensitive Restricted strictly to files created by StudentOS (such as your Master Assignment Tracker Sheet) or files you explicitly select via Google Picker. Cannot view other files in your Drive. No
gmail.readonly Sensitive / Restricted OPTIONAL: Only requested if you explicitly turn on "Scan Gmail". Reads email sender, date, subject, and cleaned body text to extract homework and deadlines. Never sends, deletes, or modifies emails. Yes (sender, subject, and max 1,500 characters of cleaned text sent to Gemini/Groq)

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use Disclosure)

StudentOS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • No Generalized AI Model Training: Google Workspace user data (including Gmail message content and Classroom announcements) received via Google APIs is NEVER used to train, retrain, or fine-tune generalized machine learning or artificial intelligence models.
  • No Advertising: User data received through Google APIs is never used or transferred to serve advertisements, including personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising.
  • No Data Brokering or Sale: We never sell Google user data or transfer it to third parties for commercial brokering.
  • No Human Reading: No human reads your email or coursework content unless: (a) you provide explicit consent for technical troubleshooting of a specific error; (b) required for security or abuse prevention; (c) required by applicable law; or (d) aggregated and anonymized for internal app metrics.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing Disclosures (Gemini & Groq)

StudentOS uses modern Large Language Models (LLMs) to help students parse complex Vietnamese and English teacher announcements and study materials:

  • Primary AI Provider: Google Gemini API (gemini-flash-latest) via StudentOS serverless proxy.
  • Fallback AI Provider: Groq API (Meta Llama 3.3) for high availability when Gemini is temporarily unreachable.
  • Data Sent to AI:
    • Gmail: Sender name and email, subject, timestamp, and up to 1,500 characters of cleaned email body (signatures and nested historical quotes are automatically stripped). Promotional and social emails are excluded by default.
    • Classroom Announcements: Class name and announcement body.
    • Natural Language Commands: Typed scheduling requests (e.g., "move Math to Friday") and session names.
    • AI Study Tutor: The specific study question you submit.
  • Data NEVER Sent to AI: Account passwords, Canvas API access tokens, grade transcripts, class roster contacts, or sensitive personal identification files.
  • Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: AI outputs are provisional drafts. Every assignment extracted by the AI displays an AI Confirm Card showing the exact source snippet and proposed date. Nothing is committed to your task list or calendar until you explicitly confirm it.
  • Socratic Tutor Policy: The AI study tutor is strictly governed by pedagogical guardrails: it provides step-by-step conceptual hints and guided questions; it never provides ready-to-copy exam answers or completed essays.

5. Canvas LMS & Other Academic Integrations

If your school uses Canvas LMS, you may optionally supply a read-only Canvas Calendar iCal Feed or a personal access token:

  • The Canvas API token is stored exclusively inside your local device's browser memory (localStorage) and is never transmitted to our backend database.
  • It is used solely to query enrolled assignments, submission deadlines, and course syllabus info directly between your browser and your institution's Canvas domain.

6. Data Storage, Architecture & Security

  • Client-Side Local-First Storage: Downloaded emails, timetables, study sessions, and offline assignments are stored in your device's browser storage (IndexedDB via Dexie and localStorage). If you use StudentOS offline or on a single machine, your data remains with you.
  • Firebase Authentication & Firestore: To enable sign-in across your devices, Firebase Authentication manages your login session. Encrypted Google OAuth refresh tokens are stored in Cloud Firestore under strict per-user security rules (request.auth.uid == userId). No other user or unauthorized party can read your credentials.
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your client, the StudentOS proxy, Google APIs, and AI endpoints are strictly encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.

7. Children's and Students' Privacy (COPPA, FERPA & Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP)

StudentOS is designed for middle school, high school, and university students (ages 11 and above).

  • No Commercial Profiling: We do not track student behavior across external websites or build commercial advertising profiles.
  • Educational Compliance: StudentOS operates as an academic organizer. We respect student educational records under FERPA principles and the Vietnamese Cybersecurity Law / Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP on Personal Data Protection.
  • Parental Rights: Parents or legal guardians may inspect, review, or request the immediate deletion of their child's stored data at any time by contacting buianhuy2009@gmail.com.

8. User Control, Revocation & Complete Data Deletion

You have unconditional control over your personal data:

  • In-App One-Click Wipe ("Delete all my data"): Available inside the Privacy modal in the application. Clicking this instantly deletes all local IndexedDB tables, clears browser storage, purges your encrypted OAuth refresh grant from Firebase Firestore, and logs you out.
  • Revoke Google Account Permissions: You can revoke StudentOS's access to your Google account at any time with immediate effect via Google Account Settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data Portability: Your assignments are synced to your own Google Sheet in Google Drive. You retain full ownership and export capability of your coursework independently of StudentOS.

9. Embedded Third-Party Learning Tools

StudentOS provides optional embedded STEM workspaces (Desmos Graphing, GeoGebra, PhET Interactive Simulations, WolframAlpha, Excalidraw, Canva). These widgets execute inside sandboxed <iframe> containers with strict Permissions-Policy restrictions (camera, microphone, geolocation, and payment APIs are completely blocked).

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make material changes to how we handle personal data or Google scopes, we will notify users through our in-app Changelog modal and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

4. Xử lý Trí tuệ Nhân tạo (Google Gemini & Groq)

StudentOS ứng dụng AI để giúp học sinh tự động phát hiện hạn nộp bài tập từ thư từ của nhà trường:

  • AI chính: Google Gemini API (gemini-flash-latest) qua máy chủ bảo mật của StudentOS.
  • AI dự phòng: Groq API (Meta Llama 3.3) kích hoạt tự động nếu Gemini gặp sự cố kết nối, đảm bảo ứng dụng luôn hoạt động.
  • Dữ liệu gửi cho AI:
    • Thư Gmail: Tên và địa chỉ người gửi, ngày gửi, tiêu đề, và tối đa 1.500 ký tự đầu của nội dung thư (đã cắt bỏ chữ ký và các lượt trích dẫn cũ). Thư quảng cáo và mạng xã hội tự động bị bỏ qua.
    • Thông báo Classroom: Tên môn học và nội dung thông báo.
    • Câu lệnh học tập: Câu điều chỉnh lịch học bạn gõ (ví dụ: "Dời Toán tối thứ 3 sang sáng thứ 7").
    • Gia sư AI: Nội dung câu hỏi bạn gửi trong khung trò chuyện gia sư.
  • Dữ liệu KHÔNG BAO GIỜ gửi cho AI: Mật khẩu tài khoản, mã truy cập Canvas, điểm thi/học bạ, danh bạ trường lớp, hay file tài liệu cá nhân.
  • Học sinh luôn là người duyệt cuối (Human-in-the-Loop): Kết quả từ AI chỉ là bản nháp gợi ý. Mọi bài tập AI tìm thấy đều xuất hiện trên Thẻ xác nhận AI kèm trích dẫn gốc từ thầy cô. Không có bài tập hay hạn nộp nào được lưu trước khi bạn bấm đồng ý.
  • Gia sư AI phương pháp Socratic: Gia sư AI chỉ gợi ý từng bước, giải thích khái niệm và cho bài tập tương tự; tuyệt đối từ chối giải hộ toàn bộ bài tập hoặc viết văn sẵn để học sinh chép.
