5 Desmos tricks that save 20 minutes per problem set

  1. Sliders for parameters: type y = mx + b — Desmos offers sliders. Drag to feel the math before solving.
  2. Restrict domains: y = x^2 {x > 0} plots half the parabola. Perfect for piecewise functions.
  3. Tables + regression: paste lab data into a table, then y1 ~ ax1 + b fits the line and shows R².
  4. Scientific mode for units: check arithmetic before writing the final solution.
  5. Snapshot to notes: screenshot the graph into your lab report or essay outline in one click.

Open StudentOS → Create → Desmos and try trick #1 on your current set. It takes 60 seconds.
