Canvas vs Google Classroom: which should you connect?

Short answer: connect both. They cover different classes, and StudentOS merges them into one list so nothing slips.

Canvas (2 minutes)

Paste your Calendar Feed URL (Canvas → Calendar → Calendar Feed). You get assignments, quizzes and exam dates — no password needed. If your school enables API tokens, add one for grades and one-click submit receipts.

Google Classroom (1 click)

Sign in with Google and pick your account. Coursework titles and due dates appear next to Canvas items, matched by name + date so nothing duplicates.

What students ask

Will it submit work for me? No — submitting always needs your tap, and you get a receipt. Does it read my personal email? Only school mail, read-only, on your device.
