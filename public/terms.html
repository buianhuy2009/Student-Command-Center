2. Description of the Service

StudentOS is an academic operating platform that aggregates course assignments, timetables, and teacher announcements from Google Classroom, Canvas LMS, and Gmail into a consolidated dashboard. Features include:

  • Coursework deadline aggregation and calendar synchronization.
  • Bi-directional syncing with personal Google Sheets.
  • Socratic AI study assistance and natural language timetable scheduling.
  • Offline-capable PWA tools (calculators, flashcards, pomodoro timers, STEM simulators).
