## 2. Service Description
StudentOS is a free and open-source academic dashboard designed to help students organize coursework, manage schedules, and study effectively:
- Coursework deadline aggregation from Google Classroom, Canvas LMS, and Gmail.
- Bi-directional synchronization with personal Google Sheets and Google Calendar.
- Socratic AI study assistance and natural language timetable scheduling.
- Offline-capable STEM simulators, flashcards, Pomodoro timers, and academic utilities.
